# Aadesh Upadhyay
Hi there! I'm Aadesh Upadhyay. I work in the intersection of LLM evaluation and LLM read-teaming and cybersecurity. My research interests lie in: Vision Language Models, Small Language Models, Computer Vision and Image Segmentation.


About me:
I am a machine learning researcher and engineer with over 3 years of experience implementing and training state-of-the-art machine learning models.

My research endeavors are driven by a commitment to enhancing the performance of AI models. It is my vision to facilitate the deployment of these models in real-world scenarios. By developing visually intuitive explanations of complex machine learning models, my goal is to make AI interpretable and trustworthy enough for use in practical applications.
